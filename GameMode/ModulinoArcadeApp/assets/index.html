        How to play

        Reaction: after the countdown, wait for a button's LED (and that third of the LED matrix) to light, then press that button as fast as you can. 5 rounds. Pressing early, late or the wrong button adds 1000 ms.

        Simon: watch the lights and tones, then repeat the sequence on the buttons (A = left, B = middle, C = right). It grows by one each round.
